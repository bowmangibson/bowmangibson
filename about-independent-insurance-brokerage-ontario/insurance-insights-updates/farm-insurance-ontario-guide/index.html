Farm Insurance in Ontario: A Plain-Language Guide for Durham Region Farming Families

Published 2025-04-25 · Bowman & Gibson Insurance Brokers · RIBO Licensed

Farm insurance is genuinely more complex than personal or standard commercial coverage. A farm policy needs to cover an unusual combination of property, liability, equipment, and sometimes livestock - in a single integrated package. Getting it right matters.

What Makes Farm Insurance Different

A farm operation has insurance needs that don't fit neatly into any single standard category. The farm dwelling is a home, but it sits in the middle of a business operation. Farm equipment is commercial property with unique characteristics. Livestock is a living asset. Agricultural liability has exposures that don't appear in standard business policies.

Farm Buildings and Structures

Barns, stables, implement sheds, grain storage, and outbuildings all need coverage. Key considerations include replacement cost vs. actual cash value for older structures, code upgrade coverage for rebuilds, and vacancy provisions for seasonal structures.

Farm Liability in Ontario

Farm liability exposure includes premises liability, products liability if you sell produce or food products directly, pollution liability, custom farming operations, and employer liability. Standard farm liability starts at $2M. Many Durham Region operations should carry $5M given the potential magnitude of agricultural liability claims.

Government Programs

AgriStability and AgriInvest programs complement but do not replace commercial farm insurance for property and liability risks. We can help you understand how to coordinate these programs with your commercial coverage.
