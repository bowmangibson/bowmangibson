Why Ontario Has the Highest Auto Insurance Rates in Canada - and What You Can Do About It

Published 2025-02-10 · Bowman & Gibson Insurance Brokers · RIBO Licensed

Ontario has had the highest auto insurance rates in Canada for over a decade. The average premium now exceeds $2,000 per year - roughly double what drivers in Quebec and Alberta pay. Understanding why, and what you can actually control, is the first step.

Why Ontario Rates Are So High

Several structural factors drive Ontario premiums: generous statutory accident benefits (SABS), fraud and inflated claims particularly in the GTA, high litigation costs, and significant catastrophe exposure from severe weather events.

What You Can Actually Control

  • Driving record - at-fault accidents and convictions affect your rate for 6 years
  • Vehicle choice - check rates before buying a new vehicle
  • Annual kilometres - low-mileage drivers should ask about usage-based insurance programs
  • Bundling - home and auto with the same carrier saves 5-15%
  • Winter tires - a 2-5% discount for four winter tires costs almost nothing to obtain
