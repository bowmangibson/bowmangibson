Ontario Motorcycle Insurance: Everything You Need to Know for the Season

Published 2026-03-10 · Bowman & Gibson Insurance Brokers · RIBO Licensed

Motorcycle insurance in Ontario is more complex and often more expensive than riders expect, particularly for newer or younger riders. Understanding the coverage environment before you get on the road is worth the time.

Why Ontario Motorcycle Insurance Is Expensive

Motorcyclists are dramatically more likely to be seriously injured in an accident than car occupants. Accident benefits - medical, rehabilitation, income replacement - account for the majority of the premium cost, not physical damage to the bike. Newer and less experienced riders are statistically much more likely to have accidents.

Seasonal Storage and Premiums

Seasonal storage savings are more modest than expected because accident benefits exposure accrues year-round. Some insurers offer winter storage endorsements - ask specifically about this when comparing quotes.

How Your Bike Affects Your Rate

Higher displacement, sport bike style, newer and higher-value bikes all increase premiums. Your riding experience and driving record are major factors. Sport bikes carry higher theft rates and repair costs.

Available Discounts

MTO-approved safety course completion, experienced rider discount (M2 for 3+ years), multi-vehicle discount, winter storage endorsement, and anti-theft devices all provide premium reductions.
