Our story

over 100 years of independent advice in Durham Region.

Bowman & Gibson was founded in Whitby in 1922. For over a century we've been placing insurance coverage for Durham Region families, farms, and businesses - independently, without exclusive insurer contracts, and without ownership ties to any carrier.

When we recommend a carrier or a coverage option, it's because it's right for your situation - not because it pays us the highest commission or because a parent company requires it.
