Home insurance in Durham Region

Durham Region has specific home insurance considerations that a national call centre isn't positioned to advise on properly:

  • Older housing stock - many homes in Whitby and Oshawa were built between 1950 and 1990 with knob-and-tube wiring, aging plumbing, or oil heating. Each affects insurability and premium, and some insurers require updates as a condition of coverage
  • Overland flood and sewer backup risk - Durham Region has experienced significant flooding near the Lynde Creek watershed, Oshawa Creek, and other watercourses. Both endorsements are essential for most homes in flood-prone areas
  • Rising rebuild costs - construction costs in Durham Region have increased significantly since 2020. Many policies haven't been updated to reflect current rebuild values - we check this at every review
  • Rural and semi-rural properties - Clarington, Scugog, and north Durham properties have different underwriting considerations including distance from fire protection, well water, septic systems, and agricultural exposures

Auto insurance in Durham Region

Durham Region is commuter country. Many residents drive to Toronto, Markham, or Scarborough daily - which affects their auto insurance profile. Highway 401, Highway 407, and the GO Transit network all intersect with how Durham residents insure their vehicles.

  • Commuter profiles - annual mileage, commute distance, and vehicle use all affect your auto insurance rate. We make sure your policy reflects how you actually drive
  • Multi-vehicle households - many Durham Region families have two or more vehicles. Bundling them with the same carrier typically generates meaningful multi-vehicle discounts
  • Home and auto bundling - combining home and auto with the same carrier saves 5-15% on both policies. Most clients benefit from reviewing this at renewal
  • Young drivers - adding a young driver in your household is a disclosure requirement in Ontario, not optional. We help families manage the cost while keeping coverage in place
