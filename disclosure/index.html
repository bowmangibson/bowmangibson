Broker Disclosure

Our RIBO-required broker disclosure.

Bowman & Gibson Insurance Brokers - as required by the Registered Insurance Brokers of Ontario.

Bowman & Gibson Insurance Brokers is licensed by the Registered Insurance Brokers of Ontario (RIBO) and is required to provide this disclosure to clients. This disclosure outlines how we are compensated, how we manage potential conflicts of interest, and your rights as a client.

Compensation Disclosure

Bowman & Gibson Insurance Brokers may receive compensation from insurers, MGAs, wholesalers, or other insurance markets. Compensation may include commission, contingent commission, or other compensation arrangements, where applicable. In most cases, our compensation is paid by the insurer as part of the premium.

If a separate brokerage fee applies to your transaction, it will be disclosed to you in advance of binding coverage.

Who we are

Bowman & Gibson Insurance Brokers is operated through Chahrouri Holdings Inc., a RIBO-licensed insurance brokerage located at 145 Brock Street South, Whitby, Ontario L1N 4J9. We have operated as an independent brokerage since 1922.

Independence

Bowman & Gibson is an independent brokerage. We are not owned by, affiliated with, or under the control of any insurance company or insurer group. We access markets from multiple insurance carriers and are not obligated to place coverage with any specific carrier.

How we are compensated

Insurance brokers in Ontario are compensated by the insurance companies whose products they sell. Our compensation takes the following forms:

  • Commission - a percentage of the premium you pay, paid to us by the insurer
  • Contingency or profit sharing - additional compensation that some insurers pay based on the overall profitability of the business we place with them
  • Volume bonuses - compensation based on the volume of business placed with a specific carrier in some arrangements

You are not charged separately for our brokerage services. Our fees are included in the commission paid by the insurer. Where a separate brokerage fee is charged, it will be disclosed to you in advance and with your consent.

Potential conflicts of interest

Our compensation structure creates the following potential conflicts of interest:

  • Carriers that pay higher commissions or contingency arrangements may create an incentive to place coverage with those carriers even where another carrier might offer better terms for your specific situation
  • Volume-based arrangements with specific carriers may create an incentive to direct business to those carriers

We manage these conflicts by placing coverage based on what we believe is best for the client - on coverage terms, price, and carrier claims service - not on the basis of our compensation from any specific carrier. Our status as an independent brokerage means we are never obligated to place coverage with any single carrier.

Your rights

  • You have the right to request information about our compensation arrangements with any specific insurer
  • You have the right to request that we place your coverage with a specific carrier, subject to that carrier's underwriting requirements
  • You have the right to make a complaint. Complaints should first be directed to our office. If unresolved, complaints may be directed to RIBO at ribo.com
