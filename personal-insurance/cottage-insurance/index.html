Cottage & Seasonal Insurance

Coverage for your cottage when you're not there.

Seasonal properties have different risk profiles than year-round homes. Vacancy periods, limited access, waterfront exposure, and older construction all affect how your policy should be structured.

Seasonal or Year-Round Coverage

Whether your cottage is occupied only in summer or used year-round, coverage terms need to reflect actual occupancy. Vacancy provisions in standard home policies often exclude or limit claims during extended absences.

Waterfront & Dock Coverage

Docks, boathouses, and waterfront structures are typically not covered under standard property policies without specific endorsements. We make sure these are accounted for.

Water & Freeze Damage

Seasonal properties that are winterized and vacant are exposed to freeze damage risk. Proper winterization requirements, heat maintenance clauses, and water damage coverage need to be clearly defined in your policy.

Liability & Recreational Activities

Guests, swimming, boating, fires, and general recreational activities create liability exposure at seasonal properties. Adequate liability limits and proper policy structure are important for any cottage owner.
