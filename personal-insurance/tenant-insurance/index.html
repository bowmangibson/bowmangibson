Tenant Insurance

Your landlord's insurance doesn't cover you.

Your landlord's property policy covers the building. It covers nothing you own. Tenant insurance is typically one of the most affordable coverages available - and one of the most commonly skipped.

Common questions
Your landlord's insurance covers the building only. If there's a fire, your landlord's insurer pays to repair the building. Nobody pays for your furniture, your laptop, your clothes, or your temporary housing - unless you have your own policy.
Tenant insurance is typically one of the most affordable personal insurance products available. Exact premiums depend on your contents value, location, and coverage options - but the cost is usually modest relative to the protection it provides. Call us for a specific number.
Personal property stolen from your vehicle is generally covered under your tenant policy's personal property section, subject to your deductible and any sublimits for property away from your residence. Confirm the specific terms with your broker.
Coverage details
Personal Property Coverage

Everything you own - furniture, electronics, clothing, appliances, jewellery. Replacement cost coverage means you get new equivalents, not depreciated values.

Personal Liability

If you accidentally cause damage to the building, or someone is injured in your unit and sues you, your liability coverage responds. Many landlords now require proof of tenant insurance before you move in.

Additional Living Expenses

If your rental unit becomes uninhabitable after a covered loss - fire, water damage - this pays for a hotel, temporary rental, and increased living costs until you can return or find a new place.
