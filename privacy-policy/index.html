Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information.

Bowman & Gibson Insurance Brokers - 145 Brock Street South, Whitby, ON. RIBO licensed.

Get a Free Quote

Bowman & Gibson Insurance Brokers (operated through Chahrouri Holdings Inc.) is committed to protecting the privacy of clients, prospective clients, and visitors to our website. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable Ontario privacy legislation.

What information we collect

We collect personal information that is necessary to place and service insurance on your behalf. This may include:

  • Name, address, phone number, and email address
  • Date of birth and driver's licence information (for auto insurance)
  • Property details, vehicle information, and business information
  • Claims history and prior insurance information
  • Financial information where required for premium financing
  • Other information relevant to placing and servicing your insurance coverage

How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Obtain insurance quotations on your behalf from our carrier partners
  • Place and administer your insurance coverage
  • Process claims and renewals
  • Comply with regulatory obligations as a RIBO-licensed brokerage
  • Communicate with you about your coverage and related matters

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We share information with insurance carriers, reinsurers, and other parties only as necessary to place and service your coverage.

Disclosure to third parties

Personal information may be shared with:

  • Insurance carriers and underwriters for the purpose of obtaining and maintaining coverage
  • Adjusters and legal professionals in the event of a claim
  • Regulatory bodies including RIBO, FSRA, and others as required by law
  • Service providers who support our brokerage operations under appropriate confidentiality obligations

Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, to service your insurance program, and to comply with legal and regulatory obligations. RIBO-licensed brokerages are subject to specific record retention requirements.

Your rights

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you and to request corrections to inaccurate information. To make a request, contact us at the address below.

Website

Our website may collect standard server log information (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and analytics purposes. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

Contact

Questions or requests regarding this policy should be directed to: Bowman & Gibson Insurance Brokers 145 Brock Street South, Whitby, ON L1N 4J9 (905) 668-5823 · info@bowmangibson.com
