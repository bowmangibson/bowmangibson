Winter Contractor Coverage

High-risk season. High-quality coverage.

Snow removal contractor insurance is one of the most challenging commercial lines to place in Ontario. Slip-and-fall liability exposure is significant, seasonal operations compress the loss period, and some carriers have significantly restricted their appetite for this class. Snow removal contractors face a concentrated liability exposure - all of their work happens in a short window, under pressure, often in conditions that create immediate slip-and-fall risk. Claims can be serious and costly. The insurance market for snow removal has tightened over the years, making carrier access and policy terms more important than ever. Working with a broker who actively places this class is worth the conversation.

Coverage areas
Slip & Fall Liability

The primary exposure for snow removal contractors. A property that is plowed or sanded but still results in a slip-and-fall generates a claim against the contractor. Limits, coverage triggers, and contract language all matter enormously.

Commercial Auto & Equipment

Trucks, plows, salters, and loaders. All commercial vehicles need proper commercial auto coverage - personal policies exclude business use. Equipment on job sites needs separate coverage.

Contract Review Awareness

Many snow removal contracts attempt to transfer significant liability to the contractor. We strongly recommend a legal review of your service contracts to understand what you're accepting before you sign. Your insurance should align with your contractual obligations.

Equipment Breakdown

A plow truck breakdown in the middle of a winter storm can trigger claim exposure if contracted properties aren't serviced. Equipment breakdown coverage and business continuity planning both matter for snow removal operators.

Why use a broker for commercial insurance

Snow removal is a high-claims class. The liability exposure is concentrated, the work is done under pressure in hazardous conditions, and the contracts often favour the property owner. An independent broker who understands this market can find coverage where others can't and structure the policy correctly for the specific operations involved.
