Liability Insurance

The coverage every Ontario business needs first.

CGL protects against third-party injury, property damage, and advertising claims arising from your operations. Most contracts and leases require it - we make sure your limits are actually adequate.

Who this covers

Built for Ontario business owners
who need proper commercial advice.

CGL policies look similar on the surface but differ significantly in how they define covered operations, exclude certain activities, limit coverage in certain jurisdictions, and handle additional insured requirements. A policy that looks adequate for your business today may not respond correctly to a specific claim because of how it's worded.

Coverage areas
Premises & Operations Liability

Covers third-party claims arising from your business premises and ongoing operations - slips and falls, accidental property damage, and bodily injury caused by your business activities.

Products & Completed Operations

Covers claims arising from products you sell, manufacture, or distribute - and from work you've already completed. Critical for contractors, manufacturers, food businesses, and product retailers.

Personal & Advertising Injury

Covers claims for libel, slander, copyright infringement, false arrest, and invasion of privacy arising from your business operations or advertising.

Medical Payments

Pays for minor medical expenses for third parties injured on your premises, regardless of fault - often used to resolve small claims without litigation.

Why use a broker for commercial insurance

The right CGL limit for your business depends on your contracts, your industry, your revenue, and your specific operations. Many businesses discover their limits are inadequate only when a claim is filed. An independent broker reviews your contractual obligations and operational exposures to recommend a limit structure that actually protects your business.

Common questions
Minimum $2M is standard for most Ontario commercial leases and vendor contracts. Many businesses carry $5M or more. Your contracts will often specify a minimum - read them carefully and ensure your policy meets those requirements.
An additional insured is a party added to your policy who receives certain protections under it. General contractors, property landlords, municipalities, and clients commonly require additional insured status on your CGL policy as a condition of doing business. We handle the endorsements.
Standard CGL policies exclude professional liability - errors made in the course of providing professional services. If you provide advice, design, consulting, or other professional services, a separate Professional Liability / E&O policy is needed.
