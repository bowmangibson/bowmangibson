Commercial Auto & Fleet

Fleet insurance for every vehicle your business needs.

One work truck or a hundred - commercial auto policy covers the vehicles your business depends on, whether you own them, lease them, or your team drives their own.

Who this covers

Built for Ontario business owners
who need proper commercial advice.

Commercial auto rating is more complex than personal auto - it accounts for vehicle use, driver lists, route exposure, cargo, and loss history across the fleet. A broker with commercial auto experience works to ensure your fleet is properly scheduled, drivers are properly disclosed, and your limits reflect the actual liability exposure of your vehicles.

Coverage areas
Fleet Third-Party Liability

Mandatory liability coverage for all commercial vehicles. We recommend $2M+ for most commercial fleets - the minimum $200K is inadequate for any serious commercial incident.

Collision & Comprehensive

Physical damage coverage for your fleet vehicles. Particularly important for financed or leased vehicles, and for any fleet where vehicles have significant value.

Cargo Coverage

If you transport goods for hire or your own inventory, cargo coverage protects the load in transit. Limits and exclusions vary significantly - we structure coverage around what you actually carry.

Non-Owned & Hired Auto

Covers liability when employees use personal vehicles for business purposes or when you rent vehicles. Often overlooked - and a significant gap in many business insurance programs.

Why use a broker for commercial insurance

Commercial auto claims can be expensive. A single serious accident involving a commercial vehicle can generate liability claims well in excess of basic limits. Fleet programs benefit from a broker who understands commercial auto underwriting, manages the driver disclosure requirements, and reviews coverage annually as the fleet changes.
