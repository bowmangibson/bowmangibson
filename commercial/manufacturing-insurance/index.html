Industrial Coverage

Insurance for what your operation actually produces.

Manufacturing insurance in Ontario must account for a risk profile that goes beyond a standard commercial package - heavy equipment, product liability for goods manufactured and sold, business interruption exposure for a production-dependent operation, and environmental considerations depending on the industry.

Who this covers

Built for Ontario business owners
who need proper commercial advice.

Manufacturing operations have significant concentrations of risk - a single equipment breakdown or fire in a production facility can generate property, business interruption, liability, and supply chain claims simultaneously. The right manufacturing insurance program is built around the specific operation, the products manufactured, and the contractual obligations to clients and suppliers.

Coverage areas
Industrial Property Coverage

Buildings, machinery, raw materials, work-in-progress inventory, and finished goods. Manufacturing property valuations are complex - accurate limits require a proper assessment of machinery and equipment values.

Products Liability

Coverage for claims arising from products you manufacture and distribute. This is one of the most significant exposures for any manufacturer. Recall costs, product defect claims, and completed operations coverage all fall under this category.

Equipment Breakdown

When production equipment fails, the cost isn't just repair - it's lost production, missed delivery obligations, spoiled inventory, and potential client claims. Equipment breakdown coverage addresses the full cost of a machinery failure.

Environmental Liability

Depending on the manufacturing process, environmental liability may be a relevant exposure. Pollution and environmental liability endorsements are available for operations with chemical, waste, or emissions exposure.

Why use a broker for commercial insurance

Manufacturing operations are unique enough that a generic commercial package rarely provides the right coverage at the right limits. Product liability terms, equipment scheduling, business interruption calculation methods, and environmental considerations all need to be tailored to the specific manufacturing operation. Independent broker advice ensures the program fits the operation.

Common questions
Manufacturing business interruption is typically calculated based on gross profit - revenue minus variable costs - for the projected period of interruption. The calculation needs to account for production capacity, backlog, and supply chain recovery time. Getting this right before a loss is critical.
Standard product liability policies cover claims for bodily injury or property damage caused by your products. Voluntary or government-ordered product recalls are usually a separate coverage - Product Recall Insurance. If your products carry meaningful recall risk, this coverage is worth discussing.
Equipment Breakdown Insurance (EBI) covers the sudden and accidental breakdown of machinery and electrical equipment - things like motors, pumps, compressors, and production equipment. It covers repair costs, replacement equipment, spoiled inventory, and business interruption caused by the breakdown. It is not the same as property insurance.
