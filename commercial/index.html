What commercial insurance typically covers

Most Ontario businesses need some combination of the following coverage lines. The right program depends on your operation:

Commercial General Liability - Third-party bodily injury, property damage, and completed operations
Commercial Property - Buildings, contents, inventory, and tenant improvements
Business Interruption - Lost income and ongoing expenses during a covered shutdown
Commercial Auto / Fleet - Vehicles used for business - personal policies exclude commercial use
Tools & Equipment - Tools, mobile equipment, and contractor equipment on and off site
Professional Liability / E&O - Advice, design, and service-based errors and omissions
Cyber Liability - Data breaches, ransomware, and business email compromise
Equipment Breakdown - Mechanical failure of boilers, machinery, and electrical equipment
Commercial Crime - Employee theft, robbery, and fraudulent transfer
Umbrella / Excess Liability - Limits above underlying policies - required by many contracts and clients

Why broker advice matters for commercial insurance

Commercial insurance markets are not consumer-friendly. Policies differ in how they define covered operations, how they handle additional insured requirements, what exclusions apply, and how claims are adjusted. Most business owners don't discover these differences until a claim is denied or a certificate doesn't meet a client's requirements.

An independent broker works for you. We review your contracts, identify coverage gaps before they matter, manage your certificates, and advocate on your behalf at claims time. We access 30+ markets and place your program with whoever is the best overall fit - not the carrier with the highest commission.
