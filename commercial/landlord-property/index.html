Landlord & Property Insurance

Protect the properties you've worked hard to own.

Residential rentals, multiplexes, and commercial property. Structured to account for tenant risk, vacancy exposure, and your mortgage lender's requirements.

Residential Rental Property

Coverage for single-family homes, semis, and townhomes rented to tenants. Accounts for tenant occupancy, which changes the risk profile from owner-occupied.

Multiplex & Apartment Buildings

Coverage for duplexes, triplexes, fourplexes, and larger apartment buildings. Fire insurance is mandatory under most Ontario mortgage agreements.

Commercial Property

Standalone or mixed-use commercial buildings. Vacancy rate, tenant mix, and construction type are key underwriting factors.

Premises Liability

Protects you as a property owner from slip-and-fall claims, injury on common areas, and other premises liability exposures.
